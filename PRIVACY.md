# Privacy Policy

Effective: July 28, 2026

Home Assistant Agent for SteamVR processes SteamVR device, application, and
connection information on your Windows PC and makes it available over a local
WebSocket server to Home Assistant clients that you choose to connect.

The processed information may include:

- local network addresses and the computer or device name;
- SteamVR connection and process status;
- the name of the running VR application;
- headset activity and controller status; and
- settings that you configure in the app.

The app does not send this information to the developer. It does not include
analytics or advertising, and the developer does not sell or share data with
third parties. Data remains on your devices and local network unless you
configure other software or networking to transmit it elsewhere.

App settings are stored locally by Windows. Windows backup features may back up
app data according to your Windows and Microsoft account settings. You can stop
processing by closing or uninstalling the app.

For support or privacy questions, open an issue at
https://github.com/Antoni-Czaplicki/SteamVR.HA-Agent/issues.
